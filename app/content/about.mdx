---

<span className="text-ink-blue font-medium">Rollick Carlberg West</span> has 7
years of experience and a deep love for the game. He believes peer-to-peer
connection is vital for kids. Having a "third space" that is neither school nor
home is essential for growth.

He provides a safe, inclusive environment where every player, including
neurodivergent kids, feels welcomed and heroic. His table is a place where math
meets magic, and shy kids become bold leaders.
